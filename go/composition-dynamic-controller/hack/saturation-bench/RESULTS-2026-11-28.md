# cdc CPU-saturation gate, off vs on: results (2026-11-28)

Harness: `run.sh` in this directory, run once end to end (12:16 to 14:39 UTC, `date -u`). Raw data:
`results/20261128T121628Z/` (per run: `cadvisor.csv`, `gate.csv`, `prom-*.txt`, `ready.json`, `meta.json`, `gate-log.txt`,
`pod-status.json`; `runs.csv` and `summary.md` are the analysed output). The cdc's full logs (12 MB) are not committed;
`gate-log.txt` holds every gate line from them.

## Verdict, in one paragraph

The gate engaged in every on run, so the comparison is not vacuous. Under a burst of 150 compositions at a 250m CPU limit
with 16 workers, turning the gate on made the burst take **9.4% longer to reach all-Ready** (254.3 s off, 278.3 s on, n=3 each,
ranges 253-257 vs 273-282, no overlap), cut container CPU throttling from 98.1% to 87.8% of periods and mean cpu.pressure
from 57.6% to 50.0%, and left the amount of work done (CPU used, reconciles completed) unchanged within noise. Per-reconcile
latency cannot be separated between arms with the metric available (the histogram's buckets are too coarse, see (b)). At light
load the gate never cut capacity (0 cuts, 0 of 306 gate samples below 16).

## Positive control: the instrument sees pressure and the gate engaged

- The kubelet exports `container_pressure_cpu_waiting_seconds_total` for the cdc container (preflight in the run: 79 series on the cdc's node), so
  PSI is available and the gate was not "disabled, no cgroup v2/PSI".
- Pressure was present in every burst: whole-burst mean 49.0-58.7%, and 7-25 of 21-32 thirty-second windows over the gate's
  threshold of 50 in every run (both arms).
- ON runs 2, 3, 6: the gate's own `unstructured_runtime.ratelimit.local.capacity` fell from 16 to a minimum of 1, 2 and 1 (mean 4.1-4.2
  over the burst), and its own `saturation.input` gauge peaked at 58.5%, 57.7% and 61.3% (above 50). The log line `Controller saturated:
  lowering local capacity` appeared 17, 18 and 18 times in the burst window (37, 39 and 37 over the whole run), and capacity reached the
  floor of 1 in all three. The pre-burst capacity was 16 in each (the ramp had finished).
- OFF runs 1, 4, 5: no `local.capacity` series at all (the gauge is not reported with the gate off, 0 samples), 0 cut log lines in
  the whole run. The log instrument is shown to work by the ON arm's counts above.

## (b) Per-reconcile latency

From the cdc's `unstructured_runtime_reconcile_duration_seconds` histogram, exported over OTLP every 2 s, delta between the start of the
burst and the point all 150 were Ready, Prometheus-style interpolation inside the bucket. **The resolution is the bucket, not the
number:** the histogram's bounds are 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 s at this scale (unstructured-runtime `secondsBoundaries`).

| arm | reconciles | p50 (s) | p95 (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| off (runs 1, 4, 5) | 465, 463, 478 | 39.5, 22.1, 23.8 | 219.7, 216.9, 215.5 |
| on (runs 2, 3, 6) | 469, 465, 460 | 32.3, 22.2, 24.5 | 226.6, 225.4, 227.0 |

Every p95 is inside the (100, 250] bucket and every p50 inside (10, 50]; the spread inside the off arm (22.1 to 39.5) is larger than
any off-versus-on difference. **No effect on per-reconcile latency is demonstrated, and none is excluded** at a resolution finer than
those buckets. Reconciles at this load take tens of seconds because 16 workers share 250m.

The gate lowers pressure and throttling as designed (the on arm sits near the threshold; the off arm sits at the quota) and uses about 3
points less of the CPU quota. Whole-burst means are the robust figures: cAdvisor refreshes every 10-15 s, so the per-window maxima are
approximate (windows are 30 s for that reason, and a 10 s window read 147% in a dev run, which a stall share cannot be).

## (d) Idle and light load: false throttle

Two phases per run, both with the gate in the state of that run's arm.

**Light load, 10 compositions at rest, 300 s after a 60 s grace** (the false-throttle probe): in the three on runs, **0 cuts, capacity
16 in all 306 gate samples** (93, 108, 105), maximum 30 s pressure window 12.9-14.5%, below the threshold of 50. The off runs looked
the same on the cAdvisor figures (maximum window 12.0-13.5%, throttled 27-31% in both arms). The gate did not cut capacity at light
load. This is the expected behaviour at pressure well under 50; it is not a test of a brief stall at light load, which this run did not produce.

**At rest after the burst, 150 compositions, 240 s after a 60 s grace**: this is not idle. At 250m the default-resync traffic of 150
compositions alone saturates the container (off: pressure mean 60.1-62.6%, throttled 99.5-100%, CPU 97-104% of limit). The gate cut 15-16 times
per on run here (capacity min 2, mean 4.1-4.4); those cuts answer a real saturation, not a false one. Compared with off: reconciles
completed 377-384 against 430-447 (about 14% fewer), p50 86-89 s against 71-75 s, p95 225-229 s against 97-165 s.

## What this does not show

- One cluster shape (3 x e2-standard-4), one chart (3 ConfigMaps and a pause Deployment, a cheap reconcile), one CPU limit (250m), one
  threshold (the default 50), one load shape (a single burst of creates), 16 workers, n=3 per arm. The production default is no CPU
  limit and 1 worker, where the gate has a floor of 1 and cannot cut; this measurement answers only "when the container does saturate".
- Per-reconcile latency (b) is not resolved. A finer histogram, or traces, would be needed.
- cpu.pressure and throttling come from cAdvisor, not from `kubectl exec` into the container (the image is distroless); the gate's own
  `saturation.input` gauge, which reads the cgroup file directly, was available only in the on arm.
- OTLP export every 2 s runs in both arms and costs the cdc some CPU of its own; the figures include it identically.
- The light-load phase had pressure well under the threshold throughout, so it shows no cut where none is designed, not robustness to
  a spiky stall.
- The cluster's cost is an estimate (see the README), not read from billing.
